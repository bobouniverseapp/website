BoboKnock — Delete Account

This page explains how to delete your BoboKnock account.

Local-Only Account (V1)

BoboKnock uses a local-only account system. In V1, there is no BoboKnock cloud authentication or Bobo Universe backend account for gameplay.

Account, session, and gameplay progress are stored locally on your device.

You can delete your BoboKnock account directly inside the app. You do not need to email support to delete your local account.

How to Delete Your Account in BoboKnock

  1. Open BoboKnock.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Complete the confirmation steps.
  5. Confirm the final DELETE action.

What Is Removed

Completing Delete Account in the app removes the locally stored:

  • BoboKnock account
  • session
  • gameplay progress
  • related local game data

Important Notes

  • There is no BoboKnock cloud gameplay account to delete in V1.
  • Third-party data processed independently by services such as Google AdMob is governed by those providers’ own privacy and retention policies.
  • Uninstalling the app may also remove local BoboKnock data, but the in-app Delete Account option is the recommended deletion method.
