BoboMusic — Delete Account

Last updated: November 5, 2026

This page explains how to delete your BoboMusic account and how to request deletion if you cannot open the app. BoboMusic is published by Bobo Universe.

  • App name: BoboMusic
  • Package: com.bobomusic.app
  • Developer: Bobo Universe
  • Privacy Policy: https://bobouniverseapp.github.io/website/privacy/

A. Delete Your Account in the App

The recommended method is to delete the account inside BoboMusic:

  1. Open BoboMusic.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Select Delete account.
  4. Enter your current password.
  5. Type DELETE.
  6. Confirm deletion.

Successful in-app deletion permanently removes the following account-linked information from BoboMusic:

  • the BoboMusic account and profile;
  • associated login credentials;
  • password reset records;
  • legacy account-linked cloud playlists; and
  • personal account links in referral records.

Uninstalling the app or clearing app data may remove information stored only on that device. It does not by itself delete the BoboMusic cloud account.

What remains

Deleting the BoboMusic account does not remove:

  • local playlists, search history, and audio files on the phone;
  • local Phone Pocket settings;
  • cloud backup snapshots and shared playlist codes that are not linked to the account;
  • the minimum hashed records needed to prevent trial and purchase abuse; or
  • Google Play billing records, which Google keeps under its own policies.

Referral device-lock records can also remain so the same install cannot repeat a referral. Those records do not keep the account name, email, or password.

Google Play subscriptions

Deleting a BoboMusic account does not cancel an active Google Play subscription. BoboMusic VIP keeps renewing through Google Play until you cancel it there. Deletion is not a refund.

Manage or cancel the subscription at https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions.

B. Request Deletion Outside the App

If you cannot access the app, you can ask Bobo Universe to delete the account by email. Sending the email starts a request. It does not delete the account automatically.

bobouniversereview@gmail.com

Suggested subject: BoboMusic Account Deletion Request

Send the request from the email address registered on the BoboMusic account when you can. Account ownership must be verified before the account is deleted.

Do not send your password, password-reset code, payment card details, or Google Play purchase token.

Questions

If you have questions about BoboMusic account deletion, contact bobouniversereview@gmail.com. The Privacy Policy describes how Bobo Universe apps handle information.
