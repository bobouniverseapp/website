BoboPop — Delete Account

This page explains account deletion for BoboPop. BoboPop is published by Bobo Universe.

No BoboPop Account in V1

In the current BoboPop V1 implementation, no Bobo Universe account or login is required. There is no cloud save and no cross-device progress synchronization.

Bobo Universe does not keep BoboPop gameplay data as a cloud profile, so there is no BoboPop cloud account for Bobo Universe to delete on a server.

Information Stored on the Device

Gameplay and app information is stored locally on the device. That local information may include:

  • player name;
  • best score and gameplay or progress state;
  • settings such as music, sound effects, and vibration;
  • other local state needed for gameplay or resume.

Uninstalling BoboPop or clearing the app’s storage may remove this locally stored information.

Advertising

BoboPop uses Google AdMob. Information processed by Google AdMob is governed by Google’s own privacy and retention policies. It is separate from BoboPop’s locally stored gameplay data.
