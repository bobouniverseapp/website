Delete Your BoboStop Account

This page explains how to request deletion of your BoboStop account. BoboStop is published by Bobo Universe.

How to Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your BoboStop account, send an email to:

bobouniversereview@gmail.com

Please send the request from the same email address used for your BoboStop account when possible, so we can verify ownership.

Suggested subject: BoboStop Account Deletion Request

What Will Be Deleted

After your deletion request is verified and processed, Bobo Universe will delete the following BoboStop account-linked data from our systems:

  • Your BoboStop Firebase Authentication account
  • Cloud-synced reminders associated with that account
  • Cloud-stored VIP quota / account usage data associated with that account
  • Other BoboStop account-linked backend data, if any

Local Device Data

Data stored only locally on your device is not controlled by our servers after account deletion. You can remove local BoboStop data by uninstalling BoboStop or clearing the app’s storage on your device.

Google Play Billing

Google Play purchase and subscription transaction records are managed by Google and may be retained according to Google’s own policies.

Deleting your BoboStop account does not automatically cancel an active Google Play subscription. If you have an active subscription, cancel it through Google Play separately.

Retention

Account-linked BoboStop data will be deleted after your deletion request is verified and processed, except where retention is required for legal or security reasons.

Questions

If you have questions about BoboStop account deletion, contact: bobouniversereview@gmail.com
